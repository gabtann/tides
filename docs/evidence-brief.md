## Fields

### `ticker`

Ticker of the investigated company.

### `signal`

The signal that triggered the investigation.

### `observed`

Facts directly supported by the retrieved evidence.

### `compared`

Relevant comparisons with historical data, peers, or other available evidence.

### `interpreted`

Evidence-based interpretation of the observed and compared information.

### `unknown`

Important information that cannot be established from the available evidence.

### `evidenceStrength`

Indicates the strength of the available evidence.

Allowed values:

- `Strong`
- `Moderate`
- `Weak`

This is an evidence assessment, not an investment recommendation or probability score.

### `limitation`

Important limitations affecting the investigation or interpretation.

## Evidence Principles

1. Observed facts must be distinguishable from interpretation.
2. Missing evidence must remain explicit.
3. The Agent must not fabricate unavailable information.
4. The Evidence Brief must not produce direct buy, sell, or hold recommendations.
5. Evidence strength must reflect the available evidence, not investment confidence.
