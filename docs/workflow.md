# TIDES Workflow

TIDES follows a research-oriented workflow:

WATCHLIST
↓
SCAN
↓
DETECT CHANGES
↓
TRIAGE SIGNALS
↓
INVESTIGATE
↓
CHALLENGE SIGNAL
↓
EVIDENCE BRIEF
↓
HUMAN JUDGMENT

## 1. Watchlist

The researcher defines the stocks they want TIDES to monitor.

## 2. Scan

TIDES retrieves relevant market data from Sectors.

## 3. Detect Changes

TIDES identifies potentially meaningful changes in the monitored stocks.

## 4. Triage Signals

Detected changes are prioritized based on their research relevance.

Research priority does not represent an investment recommendation.

## 5. Investigate

The AI agent selects and retrieves additional evidence, such as historical context, peer context, and fundamental information when available.

## 6. Challenge Signal

TIDES tests whether the initial signal remains meaningful after additional context is considered.

For example, a stock movement may appear unusual initially but become less significant if similar movements are observed among its peers.

## 7. Evidence Brief

TIDES summarizes the investigation into an evidence-based research brief.

The brief separates observed data, comparisons, interpretations, and uncertainties.

## 8. Human Judgment

The final interpretation and decision remain with the human researcher.
