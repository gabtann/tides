# Problem

Financial markets generate a large amount of data every day.

For stock researchers, the challenge is not simply accessing market data, but identifying which changes are worth investigating further.

A researcher may monitor many stocks at once, making it difficult to manually track unusual movements, historical context, peer movements, and relevant fundamental changes.

TIDES addresses this problem by acting as an AI research agent that helps researchers identify and investigate market movements that deserve further attention.

TIDES does not provide buy or sell recommendations. It provides evidence-based research support so the final judgment remains with the human researcher.
