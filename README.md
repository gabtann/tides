# TIDES

## AI Research Agent for Stocks

TIDES is an AI research agent designed to help stock researchers identify market movements that deserve further investigation.

Instead of asking researchers to manually analyze every stock in their watchlist, TIDES scans market data, detects potential changes, prioritizes research signals, investigates relevant context, and produces an evidence-based research brief.

### Core Workflow

Watchlist  
↓  
Scan  
↓  
Detect Changes  
↓  
Triage Signals  
↓  
Investigate  
↓  
Challenge Signal  
↓  
Evidence Brief  
↓  
Human Judgment

### What TIDES Does

- Monitors stocks in a researcher's watchlist
- Detects potentially meaningful market changes
- Prioritizes changes for further research
- Investigates historical and contextual evidence
- Challenges the initial signal using additional context
- Produces an evidence-based research brief
- Communicates uncertainty when available data is insufficient

### What TIDES Does Not Do

TIDES does not provide:

- Buy or sell recommendations
- Automated trade execution
- Investment advice
- Guaranteed predictions of future prices

TIDES is designed as a research and information tool. Final judgment remains with the human researcher.

## Data

TIDES uses Sectors as a core financial market data source.

The prototype is demonstrated using the markets and financial data currently available through Sectors.
